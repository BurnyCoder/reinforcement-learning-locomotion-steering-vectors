# Ant005 causal and implementation audit

Audit date: 2026-09-08 UTC. Scope: the existing `ant-classic-005` lateral vector, its validation and confirmation artifacts, and the published rendering path. This audit did not change the vector, strengths, environment, outcomes, selection, or success criteria. The only simulator work was replaying existing actions from an already measured validation seed; no new experimental seeds were consumed.

**The inspected implementation applies a real activation intervention that changes world-coordinate Y motion. No reward substitution, hardcoded sideways action rule, policy training, or camera-induced physical change was found. The evidence does not establish pure sideways motion relative to the robot's body, a disentangled internal concept, or a completed replicated discovery.** The locked confirmation usefulness gate failed and replication was not run.

## What was actually applied

The selected condition is `lateral`, strength `-0.1`, on the frozen pretrained SAC actor's first ReLU output, `actor.latent_pi[1]`. The vector contains 256 float32 values. Its stored norm is 5.15134954, so the applied offset has norm approximately 0.515135. Each active forward pass computes

`h_intervened = h_original - 0.1 * lateral_vector`.

The edited activation continues through the actor's second linear/ReLU layer and ordinary SAC output distribution. It is not a replacement action sequence or an externally specified Y-force. Runtime source: [architecture assertion, runtime.py:61](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L61), [actual addition, runtime.py:118](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L118), and [prediction followed by simulator step, runtime.py:212](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L212). This use of an output-edit callback agrees with the pinned [Baukit Trace implementation](https://github.com/davidbau/baukit/blob/9d51abd51ebf29769aecc38c4cbef459b731a36e/baukit/nethook.py#L65-L101). The downstream action path agrees with [SB3 2.4.1's SAC actor](https://github.com/DLR-RM/stable-baselines3/blob/v2.4.1/stable_baselines3/sac/policies.py#L147-L176).

The runtime does contain a separate constant action-bias comparator. The candidate never enters that branch: its validation and confirmation identities have `bias_sha256: null`, the same non-null vector hash, and `alpha: -0.1`. The comparator's identity instead records an action-bias hash. [experiment.py:39](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/experiment.py#L39) and [experiment.py:97](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/experiment.py#L97) dispatch these mechanisms separately. The only time rule is the registered 100-step unsteered prefix; it switches on a fixed activation offset, not a position-dependent locomotion controller.

The lateral measurement is **world Y**, calculated from the root position before and after integration divided by the actual timestep: [runtime.py:224](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L224). It is not reward, a camera coordinate, or a quaternion component. Height is actual root Z. Yaw is computed from the normalized scalar-first quaternion, with wrapped angle differences; inversion uses the sign of the body's Z axis projected onto world Z: [runtime.py:132](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L132), [runtime.py:163](../src/rl_locomotion_steering_vectors/runtime.py#L163). Forbidden contact resolves the actual named `floor` and `torso_geom` geometries, IDs 0 and 1 in this XML, and requires nonpositive contact distance. Ordinary leg-ground contact is allowed. This is a limited torso-contact/inversion proxy, not a comprehensive gait-quality classifier.

There is a deliberate distinction between the research velocity and Gymnasium's reward velocity. The former uses final root `qpos`; the original reward uses derived body `xpos`. Those arrays are not identical at every integration boundary. In the two replayed episodes the largest component difference from `info` velocity was 0.1072 and 0.1280 m/s, while the research metrics themselves reproduced exactly. No claim equates the stored physical velocity with the reward's intermediate quantity. Action effort is mean squared action, not measured energy.

The controls prevent a strong uniqueness claim. In confirmation the chosen action bias changed world Y by +0.134392 m/s, random directions by +0.155498, +0.252818, and +0.154725 m/s, and the shuffled-label vector by +0.890388 m/s. All controls failed their full gates. This is evidence that multiple internal perturbations can alter direction, not that the candidate is uniquely a lateral concept or conclusively better than every alternative. The shuffled control permutes window labels; it is a negative control, not a formal permutation test under within-episode dependence. The procedure gives equal grid opportunities, not a formal statistical comparison of candidate-versus-control superiority.

## Heading is a material interpretation limit

The fitting contrast is observational, and lateral labels were not conditioned on heading, posture, or gait phase. The independent fitting audit found correlation 0.792426 between window world-Y velocity and circular mean body heading. High versus low windows differed in heading from 0.725497 to 0.869187 rad, while their yaw-aligned planar side-velocity means changed only from −4.433902 to −4.351678 m/s. Height also differed by about 1.33 cm. The intervention identifies a causally effective activation direction, but the contrast does not isolate which correlated physical feature it represents.

A descriptive audit rotated saved horizontal velocities by the inverse yaw calculated from each pre-action quaternion. This uses yaw only, not full pitch/roll orientation, and was not used for fitting, selection, or gates. In validation, the −0.1 intervention increased world-Y velocity by 0.545011 m/s but yaw-aligned side velocity by only 0.040051 m/s; mean heading increased by approximately 0.098829 rad, or 5.66 degrees. In confirmation the corresponding side-velocity change was 0.059427 m/s while world Y changed by 0.402379 m/s. Confirmation descriptive means retain the same prefix-zero convention.

These patterns are consistent with directional/heading steering while keeping a similar body-oriented gait. They do **not** prove that heading is the sole mediator. Neither a body-relative strafing claim nor a disentangled internal “sideways” representation is established. The empirically useful sign is also opposite the extraction's high-minus-low sign: a negative activation offset raises world Y. That is compatible with closed-loop compensatory features and is another reason not to interpret the raw contrast as a direct semantic axis.

## Remaining methodological limits

- The intervention is causal for this frozen policy under the paired simulator protocol, but fitting association, sign selection, and behavior naming do not establish internal semantics. Only one pretrained policy was studied in this Ant attempt.
- Episode means include every failed episode and use its available post-onset duration. They are not a fixed 45-second outcome after an early termination. Failure rates, individual episodes, and the shared missing post-onset outcome must accompany the average velocity claim.
- The 2,000-resample episode bootstrap describes the observed paired effect. It is not a simultaneous interval over the adaptive research history or a bound proving the population failure increase is below five percentage points. The safety gate uses observed rate differences, which remain uncertain with 30 pairs.
- The cache identity hashes the runtime and treatment inputs; it does not itself hash every dependency, environment source, or analysis file. The manifest pins versions, analysis artifacts record a separate analysis hash, and this audit verified the important installed upstream sources. Those complementary records matter for reproduction.
- No replicated discovery or calibrated fresh-episode application can be claimed from this run. The software's failed status and preserved failed episodes are part of the result, not artifacts to remove.
