03 / INTERPRET & REPRODUCE

Keep the claim attached to its evidence.

What this result establishes

Adding a fixed hidden-activation vector causes a measured change in world-Y motion for the evaluated checkpoint. A change along the world’s Y axis does not by itself establish body-relative sidestepping, a clean semantic concept, or a reliable locomotion controller.

Projection ablations and the saved observation transformation support a heading-related explanation. That transformation rotates already-clipped contact wrenches too: exact physical-heading equivalence and unique heading mediation remain unestablished.
